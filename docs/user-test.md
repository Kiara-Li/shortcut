# Next task-based test

## Scenario

You know Figma and are editing a poster in Photoshop. You want to resize a layer and duplicate it, but you remember Figma’s shortcuts rather than Photoshop’s.

With Shortcut running and Accessibility enabled, find out how to scale and duplicate the layer, then complete those actions in Photoshop. Check ordinary copy once, then find one of the cards again under Saved. After reading a card, try its target shortcut within two minutes and check that it appears under Achievements. Repeat the shortcut and check that the celebration does not repeat. For the website test, use its search field to find the same two actions.

## Questions

1. Could you work out how to request a card and which shortcut to use next without an explanation from the facilitator?
2. Did the card interrupt your work, and was the difference between an ordinary shortcut and a lookup clear?
